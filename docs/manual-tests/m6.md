# Manual test session after M6

M6 added plugins. A CLAP plugin goes onto a track through a checkout, and its commit carries the
plugin's settings and a stem: the track rendered as audio. Each client decides for itself how it
hears such a track: with the plugin installed it runs it, with the committed settings; without,
it plays the stem. The automated tests do all of this with a small test synth, between three
clients and a real server, and check that the plugin and the stem sound the same. This session
is for what they cannot judge: a **real plugin** such as Vital, its **own window**, and how it
all sounds.

About 25 minutes, one machine with Vital (or another CLAP instrument) installed; section F needs
a Windows machine.

## 0. Preparation

- [ ] **0.1** Run `cargo test --workspace --exclude 'spike-*'`.
      Expect: every line says `ok`, none says `FAILED`.
- [ ] **0.2** Run `cargo build -p mdaw-server -p mdaw-client`.
      Expect: it finishes without errors.
- [ ] **0.3** Run `cargo test -p mdaw-plugin --test host -- --ignored installed --nocapture`.
      Expect: a line for each CLAP instrument installed here, Vital among them, most with a
      peak above 0. This loads every plugin once; a crash here names the culprit.

Four terminals:

```sh
cargo xtask server --fresh            # terminal 1: leave it running
cargo xtask client Ann --test-synth   # terminal 2: also offers the built-in test synth
cargo xtask client Ben                # terminal 3
cargo xtask client Cat --no-plugins   # terminal 4: loads no plugins at all
```

Put the three windows side by side. Ann clicks "+ Track" and draws a one-bar clip at bar 1 with
a few notes. Everyone selects the track.

## A. A plugin goes onto a track

- [ ] **A1** Ann: open the "Device" tab.
      Expect: "Instrument…" is greyed out, and next to it the window says that plugins go onto a
      track while you have it checked out.
- [ ] **A2** Ann: click "Check out", then "Instrument…".
      Expect: "Pocket (built in)" and the installed CLAP instruments with their makers, Vital and
      "mdaw Test Synth" among them (Ann only: the test synth is offered with `--test-synth`).
      Effects, if any are installed, are under "Add effect…".
      Cat's window says plugins are switched off there.
- [ ] **A3** Ann: pick Vital and play a few notes.
      Expect: Vital takes Pocket's place: the track has one instrument. Its panel shows the first
      parameters as sliders and "And … more in the plugin's window". Ann's notes sound through
      Vital only. Ben and Cat still hear the track as before, with Pocket.
- [ ] **A4** Ann: click "Open window".
      Expect: Vital's own window opens at its size, works, and animates. It reacts to clicks and
      drags at once, also while the transport is stopped and nothing moves in mdaw's window, and
      also while mdaw's window is covered. Closing it with the window's close button, or with
      "Close window", closes it; "Open window" opens it again.
- [ ] **A5** Ann: in Vital's window pick another preset or turn a big knob, then play the clip
      (Space).
      Expect: the new sound.

## B. Commit, and how everyone hears it

- [ ] **B1** Ann: click "Commit…".
      Expect: "Rendering" for a moment (longer for a long track), then the commit dialog with
      samples; "Play: Track alone" plays the clip as Ann hears it, Vital included.
- [ ] **B2** Ann: click "Commit". Everyone: press Home and Space.
      Expect: all three hear the clip with Ann's Vital sound. Ben, who has Vital, runs it
      himself; Cat hears the stem, and her track header says "stem". Ben and Cat sound alike;
      with Vital's own variation from note to note (it randomises phases), not bit for bit.
- [ ] **B3** Ben: open the "Device" tab and "Open window".
      Expect: Ben's Vital shows Ann's preset and settings.
- [ ] **B4** Ben: play notes on the keyboard into the track.
      Expect: Ben hears Vital at once. Ann hears Ben through her Vital; Cat hears nothing of it
      (she has no Vital to play notes with).
- [ ] **B5** Ben: try to move a note of the clip.
      Expect: refused, with the notice that a track with plugins is changed through a
      checkout.

## C. Tempo against the stem

- [ ] **C1** Ben: drag the tempo to about 90 bpm. Everyone: play.
      Expect: Ann and Ben hear the clip slower, through Vital. Cat's header says "stem, old
      tempo" in yellow; the stem plays at its old speed, out of step with the beat.
- [ ] **C2** Ben: put the tempo back to 120.
      Expect: Cat's header says "stem" again.

## D. Changing the plugin again

- [ ] **D1** Ann: check out the track, change something in Vital's window, commit.
      Expect: Ben's Vital switches to the new settings after the commit (a short break in its
      sound is expected); Cat hears the new stem.
- [ ] **D2** Ann: check out, pick "Pocket (built in)" under "Instrument…", commit.
      Expect: everyone, Cat included, hears Pocket again, and Cat's header no longer says
      "stem".

## E2. A plugin that crashes

The built-in test synth can be told to misbehave on the lowest MIDI note (0): its "Misbehave
on lowest note" setting is 0 (not at all, the default), 1 (its processing fails) or 2 (it
crashes). Ann: check out a track, pick "mdaw Test Synth" under "Instrument…", and play a few
notes on the keyboard.

- [ ] **E2.1** Ann: turn the synth's "Balance" slider to the right and "Misbehave on lowest note"
      to 2, wait three seconds, then play note 0: on a MIDI keyboard the lowest C five octaves
      below middle C, or with the computer keyboard press Z until the strip says "Octave C-1"
      and press A.
      Expect: mdaw keeps running and keeps playing everything else. A notice says "mdaw Test
      Synth crashed"; the track header says "plugin crashed"; the Device tab shows "Restart".
- [ ] **E2.2** Ann: click "Restart", then play a normal note.
      Expect: the synth plays again, still panned to the right.
- [ ] **E2.3** Repeat E2.1 with Vital's window open.
      Expect: only the test synth's helper goes; Vital and its window carry on.
- [ ] **E2.4** Ann: restart the synth, put a note 0 into the clip, set "Misbehave on lowest
      note" to 1, and click "Commit…".
      Expect: no commit dialog; a notice says the synth crashed while the commit was rendered,
      and the track stays checked out.

## E. Without a plugin installed

- [ ] **E1** Close Ben's window. Start Ben with `cargo xtask client Ben --no-plugins` and repeat
      B2 with a track that has Vital.
      Expect: Ben now hears the stem, like Cat.

## Covered by automated tests

| What | Test |
|---|---|
| A commit is not sent when a plugin fails during the render, when the render is silent, or when the track is too long for a stem | `plugins::a_commit_whose_render_goes_wrong_is_not_sent` |
| A plugin that will not start or take its settings is reported, plays the stem, and is not retried until Restart | `plugins::tests::a_plugin_that_will_not_take_its_settings_is_reported_not_played_wrong` |
| The test synth misbehaves only when told; it is offered only with `--test-synth` | `host::the_test_synth_misbehaves_only_when_told_and_a_failure_is_reported`, `clap::tests::the_test_synth_is_offered_only_when_asked_for` |
| A plugin crash leaves the session running, is reported, and the plugin restarts with its kept settings | `plugins::a_plugin_that_crashes_leaves_the_session_running_and_can_be_restarted` |
| Plugins in helper processes: same sound and state as inside; a crash costs only the helper; no waiting for a dead helper | `mdaw-plugin` `isolated` tests |
| The window keeps running while a plugin runs; plugins only through a checkout; an instrument plugin replaces Pocket; live edits of a plugin track refused; the plugin runs for its owner; commit with state and stem; another client with the plugin runs it with the committed state; a client without plays the stem and sounds the same; stale stem after a tempo change | `plugins::a_plugin_is_heard_live_where_it_is_installed_and_from_its_stem_elsewhere` |
| Picking a plugin instrument in the Device tab replaces Pocket, its settings shown, undo, Pocket picked again | `ui::a_plugin_is_added_from_the_device_tab_and_its_settings_shown` |
| "Instrument…" off on a live track with the reason shown, on after the checkout, the plugin only in the checkout | `ui_net::a_plugin_is_added_in_the_device_tab_once_the_track_is_checked_out` |
| The CLAP host: scanning, instances, notes, parameters, state saved and loaded into another instance, the processor coming back | `mdaw-plugin` `host` tests |
| Plugin devices in the audio graph: stereo, kept across rebuilds, made anew for another state, retired on the audio thread, stems aligned to the timeline | `mdaw-dsp` `external` tests |
| 24-bit FLAC for stems | `mdaw-engine` `flac::tests::stems_keep_24_bits` |

## Known gaps (not failures)

- **One instrument per track.** Effects go after it; there is no reordering yet.
- **CLAP only.** The host is built so that VST3 can be added as another backend; it is not
  there yet. Effects (plugins with an audio input) work in the device chain but have had no
  test with a real plugin.
- **Plugins run in helper processes on Linux only.** On Windows a plugin still runs inside
  mdaw, and its crash ends mdaw. A plugin that is late for a block (an overloaded machine)
  is left out of that block, which can be heard as a dropout.
- **Closing a plugin's window hides it**; the plugin's interface is torn down only when the
  plugin leaves the track or the client closes. Tearing it down is where plugins tend to crash.
- **Plugin settings reach the document only with the commit.** Turning a knob in a checkout is
  heard at once by the owner only, as with every checkout edit.
- **Anyone with the plugin can open its window**, also without a checkout. Turning its knobs
  then is heard on that machine only, and is undone by the next commit of the track.
- **The stem is the reference sound.** Clients running the plugin may sound slightly different
  where the plugin is not deterministic (Vital randomises phases).
- **A stem starts at the beginning of the timeline** and ends with the last clip. With
  "Commit and restore the old tempo" the stem is rendered at the current tempo and shows as
  stale.
- **Stems are resampled linearly** when the output device does not run at 48 kHz.
- **Live notes into a track heard from its stem make no sound**: there is no plugin to play
  them.
- **Scanning loads every plugin at start-up**, which can take a moment with many installed.
- **The plugin window** needs X11 (XWayland on Wayland desktops) on Linux.
- Everything listed in the M5 protocol under Known gaps still holds.
