# Manual test session after M4

M4 added checkout and commit: a track can be taken out of live editing, worked on alone, and
handed in as one commit that carries rendered samples everyone can play from the changelog. The
automated tests run the plan's demo between two clients and a real server, and drive the
windows headlessly through checkout, the commit dialog, the changelog and forks. This session is
only for what they cannot judge: whether the samples **sound** right, whether the locks are clear
on screen, and how the flow feels. Steps that a test now covers are listed under
[Covered by automated tests](#covered-by-automated-tests); the remaining steps keep their
numbers.

About 15 minutes, one machine.

## 0. Preparation

- [ ] **0.1** Run `cargo test --workspace --exclude 'spike-*'`.
      Expect: every line says `ok`, none says `FAILED`.
- [ ] **0.2** Run `cargo build -p mdaw-server -p mdaw-client`.
      Expect: it finishes without errors.

## B. Check out, commit, listen

Start the server with `--fresh` (it also forgets the demo profiles). Three terminals:

```sh
cargo xtask server --fresh      # terminal 1: leave it running
cargo xtask client Ann          # terminal 2
cargo xtask client Ben          # terminal 3
```

Put the two windows side by side.

Setup: Ann clicks "+ Track" twice and draws a one-bar clip at bar 1 on each track. Into the clip
of Track 1 go four short notes, one per beat; into the clip of Track 2 two long low notes.

- [ ] **B2** Ann: click "Check out" on Track 1.
      Expect: in Ann's window the button becomes a **blue** "Commit…". In Ben's window it becomes
      a **brown** "Locked", Track 1's clip is drawn **dimmed**, and the lane says "checked out by
      Ann" at its bottom left. Hovering "Locked" says Ann has it checked out. All of it reads
      clearly.
- [ ] **B6** Ann: add four notes on the off-beats of Track 1. Press Space, listen through one bar,
      press Space again.
      Expect: both windows play together, each **its own** version of Track 1: you hear the four
      old notes doubled, plus Ann's new ones once.
- [ ] **B8** Ann: click "Commit…" and, once the samples are rendered, "Play: Track alone", then
      "Play: With the others".
      Expect: the first is the bar with Ann's new notes, Track 1 only, then a short release. The
      button reads "Stop: Track alone 1.2/… s" counting up while it plays. The second plays the
      same bar with Track 2's low notes. Clicking a playing button stops it.
- [ ] **B12** Ann: click "Commit". Ben: open the "Changelog" tab and click "Play: Track alone".
      Expect: after a moment it plays **what Ann heard** in B8, also while the transport plays.

## C. Tempo changes during a checkout

- [ ] **C3** Ann: check out Track 1 and move one note. Ben: drag the tempo field down to about
      90, let go. Ann: click "Commit…", then play "Old tempo" and "Current tempo".
      Expect: the same music, the first at 120 bpm, the second slower at about 90. Then click
      "Commit and restore the old tempo".

## E. A checkout outlives its window

- [ ] **E1** Ann: check out Track 1 and add a note. Close Ann's window.
      Expect: Ben still sees Track 1 as "Locked".
- [ ] **E2** Wait a bit over a minute.
      Expect: Ben's button reads "Stale" and the lane text adds ", not heard from lately".
      Nothing else changes: the checkout is not ended by itself.
- [ ] **E3** Terminal 2: `cargo xtask client Ann`.
      Expect: within about ten seconds Ben's button is back to "Locked".

## F. "As it was": hearing an edit that lost

Setup: press Ctrl+C in terminal 1 and wait for "Reconnecting". Ann drags one note of Track 1 up a
few semitones, Ben drags the **same** note down. Terminal 1: `cargo xtask server` (without
`--fresh`), and wait until a "Play: As it was … s" button shows in the changelog.

- [ ] **F4** Click "Play: As it was" in either window.
      Expect: Track 1 alone, with the note at the pitch that **lost**.

## G. The changelog per track, with times

- [ ] **G1** Ann: open the "Changelog" tab.
      Expect: each line starts with the time of day it happened, in your time zone. Hovering over
      a time shows the full date.

## Known gaps (not failures)

- **Samples come only from built-in instruments.** Tracks with plugins and their stems come
  with M6.
- **A sample is at most 8 bars**, plus a second of release. A changed mix or device setting
  plays the first 4 bars with notes. A track without notes gets no sample ("The track is
  silent").
- **A clip of a locked track can still be dragged.** On release it jumps back and the notice
  says why.
- **One commit at a time per window.**
- **A restored fork is a new track**; the original stays as it is.
- **The changelog** of a window that just opened holds the last 200 checkouts, commits and lost
  edits; plain edits are not listed at all. Lost edits of the tempo or the project name belong to
  no track and show only under "Whole project". A fork is listed under the track it came from,
  and only under "Whole project" once that track is removed. Lines from before today show the
  date as well.
- **"As it was" comes from the window whose edit lost**, and only while it is open at that
  moment.
- **Samples are downloaded when first played** and kept in memory until the window closes. The
  server keeps every uploaded sample forever.
- **Undo after a commit**: undoing an edit made during a checkout that is committed by now acts
  as a live edit.
- **Notes you play on the piano keys are heard only in your window.** Live MIDI comes with M5.
- Everything listed in the M3 protocol under Known gaps still holds.
